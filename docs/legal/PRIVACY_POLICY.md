This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you access or use the Service, including our websites, web app, and related features.

## 1) Scope and definitions

- **“Personal Data”** means information that identifies you or can reasonably be used to identify you (directly or indirectly).
- **“Customer”** means the business or organization (or individual) that creates an account and uses the Service.
- **“End User”** means an individual authorized by a Customer to use the Service (e.g., employees, contractors).

If you are using the Service on behalf of a Customer, the Customer may control how your data is used and may have its own privacy policies.

## 2) Information we collect

### A. Information you provide

- **Account and profile information:** name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), role/permissions, and related account identifiers.
- **Organization information:** company/team name and related details you submit.
- **Content and activity data:** time entries, projects, tasks, clients, notes/descriptions, attachments or other content you choose to input.
- **Support communications:** messages, tickets, and other information you share when contacting us.

### B. Information collected automatically

- **Device and log information:** IP address, device/browser type, operating system, timestamps, pages/actions, error logs and diagnostics.
- **Cookies/local storage:** we may store session tokens and preferences (such as UI settings) using cookies and/or browser local storage.

### C. Billing and payment information

- **Payment status and transaction references:** plan, seat counts, amounts, currency, and transaction IDs needed to administer subscriptions and billing.
- **Payment instrument details:** generally handled by payment providers; we may receive limited details (e.g., last 4 digits, payment method type) depending on provider.

### D. AI feature inputs (if enabled)

If the Service provides AI-assisted features, we may process the text you submit to those features, and we may store the conversation or prompts as part of product logs or your account history depending on configuration.

## 3) How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service (including core time tracking, reporting, invoicing, and collaboration features).
- Create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and administer access and permissions.
- Process subscriptions, upgrades, seat changes, payment confirmations, fraud prevention, and billing support.
- Provide customer support and communicate with you about the Service (e.g., service notices, security alerts, and administrative messages).
- Monitor, prevent, and address security incidents, abuse, prohibited conduct, and technical issues.
- Comply with applicable laws and enforce our agreements.

## 4) Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location and applicable law, we process Personal Data on one or more of the following bases:

- **Contract/necessity:** to provide the Service you request.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure, improve, and administer the Service (balanced with your rights).
- **Consent:** where required for certain cookies or communications.
- **Legal obligation:** where we must comply with laws and lawful requests.

For users in the Philippines, we aim to process Personal Data consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and implementing rules, where applicable.

We do not sell Personal Data. We disclose information in the following scenarios:

We may share information with vendors who help us deliver the Service, such as:

These providers are permitted to process information only to perform services for us and are expected to protect it consistent with contractual requirements.

### B. Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, due diligence, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

### C. Legal and safety

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is required to:

- Comply with law, regulation, legal process, or lawful requests.
- Protect the rights, safety, and security of NexiFlow, our users, or the public.
